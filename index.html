How to Play

  1. Everyone antes 10 chips and receives five cards.
  2. Choose Check or make a small bet.
  3. Tap the cards you want to keep. Cards marked HOLD stay.
  4. Press Draw Cards to replace everything not held.
  5. After the final bet, the best poker hand wins the pot.

Every Jack is wild. Five of a kind is the highest possible hand.
